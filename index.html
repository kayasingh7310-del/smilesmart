Explore oral-health topics

Clear explanations without complicated medical language.

🦠

Cavities

Cavities form when bacteria produce acids that weaken tooth enamel.

Learn more

Brushing with fluoride toothpaste, flossing, drinking water, and limiting frequent sugar exposure can lower risk.

🩸

Gum Health

Healthy gums support your teeth and help protect the tissues underneath.

Learn more

Bleeding while brushing can be a sign of irritation. Gentle brushing and daily flossing may help, but persistent symptoms should be checked professionally.

😁

Braces

Braces make cleaning more challenging because food can collect around brackets.

Learn more

Use an orthodontic brush, clean around brackets carefully, and follow your orthodontist’s instructions.

🧵

Flossing

Floss cleans spaces between teeth that toothbrush bristles may not reach.

Learn more

Curve the floss gently around each tooth and avoid snapping it into the gums.

🥗

Nutrition

How often you consume sugar can matter as much as the total amount.

Learn more

Frequent sugary drinks or snacks repeatedly expose teeth to acid. Water, cheese, nuts, and crunchy vegetables are tooth-friendly choices.

🚨

Dental Emergencies

Severe pain, facial swelling, uncontrolled bleeding, or a knocked-out tooth need prompt attention.

Learn more

Contact a dentist or urgent medical service. This website does not diagnose or replace professional care.

Your tooth’s protective outer layer

Tip: Fluoride toothpaste helps strengthen enamel.

Our mission

Making preventive health education easier to understand.

SmileSmart is a student-created educational project designed to help teenagers understand oral health and make informed daily choices.

It combines healthcare education, technology, and interactive design to turn information into practical habits.

Educational disclaimer: SmileSmart provides general educational information only. It does not provide a diagnosis, treatment plan, or substitute for professional dental or medical care.
